# The Cosmology of the Spheres

The cosmos of the *Pistis Sophia* is densely layered. Ascending from the earth one passes through concentric regions, each governed by rulers whose authority over a soul's fate the mysteries are designed to neutralize.

## From below upward

- **The outer darkness.** A great dragon surrounding the world, containing twelve dungeons in which souls suffer for specific classes of sin. Book IV treats this region in detail.
- **The chastisements of the archons of the midst.** Intermediate correctional regions.
- **The twelve aeons of the rulers.** The classical astrological cosmos: twelve zodiacal aeons, each with its archon, its powers, and its component decans. These are the "rulers of the spheres" that Mary asks about in chapter 20.
- **The fate-sphere (*heimarmene*).** The specific mechanism by which the rulers fix the life of each soul at the moment of its descent into a body. The fate-sphere is sometimes described as a separate, further structure set up by the rulers using decrees obtained from above.
- **The thirteenth aeon.** The region of Pistis Sophia before her fall, and of the other twelve aeons of the Great Invisible Forefather.
- **The Treasury of Light.** Dwelling of the Savior, the Twin Saviors, and the Nine Guardians. Souls who have received the mysteries enter here.
- **The Midst.** A distinct region above the Treasury where the Virgin of Light, the great Sabaoth the Good, and the Seven Amens reside.
- **The Right and the Left.** Higher still, the regions of the emanations of the light.
- **The First Mystery.** Often treated as the highest named rank below the Ineffable.
- **The Ineffable.** The ultimate source; beyond names, beyond number.

This geography is not a map drawn once and held to consistently. Different chapters of the *Pistis Sophia* assign different numbers of regions and different names to the same figures. What matters to the text is the general hierarchy: below, the realm of the archons and their compulsion; above, a layered kingdom of light whose gates can only be opened by the mysteries.

## The alteration of the spheres

The single most important cosmological act of the Savior, as narrated in Book I, is the **alteration of the fate-spheres' motion**. Before Jesus's descent, the spheres turned continuously in one direction, and the archons could therefore read off, from the position of the spheres at any moment, the complete fate of any soul born at that moment.

Jesus **reversed half the rotation**: the spheres now turn six months to the left, then six months to the right. The archons can no longer read fate continuously. Astrology — the practical art of the archons' dominion — has been made unreliable.

Crucially, this is not an abolition of fate. Fate still operates, and souls that have not received the mysteries are still subject to it. But the power of fate has been **diminished by one-third**, and a window has opened for souls to escape through the mysteries that the Savior came to deliver.

## Astrology and the mysteries

The *Pistis Sophia* is one of the clearest gnostic treatments of **astrology as real but fallen**. The stars and planets of popular astrology are the archons, and their influence on human life is genuine — it is precisely the bondage from which *gnosis* delivers. A horoscope cast for a soul unprotected by the mysteries reveals its fate. A soul that has received the mysteries passes through the spheres by giving the names and seals and is no longer bound.

This structure — authentic astral determinism, broken from above by a redeeming revelation — is the cosmology within which Mary's question in chapter 20 finds its answer: souls on earth can be delivered from the rulers of the spheres because Jesus has disrupted the rulers' mechanism, and because he is about to entrust to the disciples the specific names and mysteries that complete the escape.

## The twelve aeons and the thirteenth

The number twelve is structural: twelve signs, twelve aeons, twelve rulers, twelve dungeons of the outer darkness. The **thirteenth** is consistently the region just above the twelve — the seat of Pistis Sophia before her fall, and, in some passages, the number of the first aeons of the Great Invisible Forefather. A soul that has received the mysteries passes through the twelve and leaves the thirteenth behind, rising into the regions of the Midst and the Treasury of Light.

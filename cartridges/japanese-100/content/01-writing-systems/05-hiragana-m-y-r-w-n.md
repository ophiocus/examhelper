# Chapter 5: Hiragana — M, Y, R, W Rows & N

## M-Row (ま行 まぎょう)

| Hiragana | Romaji | Example Word |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| ま | ma | まち (machi) = town |
| み | mi | みず (mizu) = water |
| む | mu | むし (mushi) = insect |
| め | me | めがね (megane) = glasses |
| も | mo | もり (mori) = forest |

## Y-Row (や行 やぎょう)

Only three characters — yi, ye don't exist in modern Japanese:

| Hiragana | Romaji | Example Word |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| や | ya | やま (yama) = mountain |
| ゆ | yu | ゆき (yuki) = snow |
| よ | yo | よる (yoru) = night |

**Important:** や, ゆ, よ are also used as small characters (ゃ, ゅ, ょ) to create combination sounds:

| Combo | Sound | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| きゃ | kya | きゃく (kyaku) = guest |
| しゅ | shu | しゅくだい (shukudai) = homework |
| ちょ | cho | ちょっと (chotto) = a little |
| にゅ | nyu | にゅうがく (nyuugaku) = enrollment |
| りょ | ryo | りょこう (ryokou) = travel |

## R-Row (ら行 らぎょう)

The Japanese "r" is a light tap, between English "r", "l", and "d":

| Hiragana | Romaji | Example Word |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| ら | ra | らいねん (rainen) = next year |
| り | ri | りんご (ringo) = apple |
| る | ru | さる (saru) = monkey |
| れ | re | れきし (rekishi) = history |
| ろ | ro | ろく (roku) = six |

## W-Row (わ行 わぎょう)

Only two characters survive in modern Japanese:

| Hiragana | Romaji | Example Word |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| わ | wa | わたし (watashi) = I/me |
| を | wo/o | (object particle only) |

**を** is always the object marker particle and pronounced "o":
- 水**を**飲む = mizu **o** nomu = drink water

## The Lone N (ん)

The only consonant that stands alone — no vowel attached:

| Hiragana | Romaji | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| ん | n | にほん (nihon) = Japan |

**ん** changes sound slightly depending on what follows:
- Before b/p/m → sounds like "m": **しんぶん** (shimbun) = newspaper
- Before n/t/d → sounds like "n": **あんない** (annai) = guidance
- Before k/g → sounds like "ng": **まんが** (manga) = manga

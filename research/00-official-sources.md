## Exam Format
- Written, open-ended questions (NOT multiple choice)
- 4 hours duration
- Block capital letters required
- Administered by Cancillería + Universidad de Antioquia
- Cities: Bogotá, Medellín, Cúcuta, Cali, Barranquilla
- Two attempts max; retake after 6 months
- Contact: examendeconocimiento@cancilleria.gov.co

## 5 Official Topics
1. Constitución Política de Colombia
2. Historia Patria
3. Geografía de Colombia
4. Cultura de Colombia (NEW - added by Ley 2332)
5. Castellano (only if Spanish is not native language)

## Exemptions
- Over 60 years old
- Completed bachiller/pregrado/postgrado in Colombia
- Cognitive disability
- Indigenous community members
- Stateless persons (apátridas)

## Cost
- 700,000 COP

## Residency Requirements (Ley 2332 de 2023)
- General: 5 years
- Spouse/partner of Colombian, parent of Colombian, or reciprocity: 2 years
- Stateless: 1 year
- NOTE: The old 1-year rule for Latin Americans (Ley 43) NO LONGER EXISTS

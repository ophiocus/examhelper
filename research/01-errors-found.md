# Errors Found During Fact-Check

## CONSTITUTION

### 02-ramas-del-poder.md
1. **Presidential reelection**: Says "no puede ser reelegido para el periodo inmediatamente siguiente" — WRONG. Acto Legislativo 02 de 2015 prohibits reelection ENTIRELY (any title, any period). Fix: clarify absolute prohibition.
2. **Consejo de Estado**: Says "27 magistrados" — INCOMPLETE. Total is 31 (27 Contencioso + 4 Consulta y Servicio Civil).
3. **Contraloría election**: Says Contralor elected from terna of three high courts — OUTDATED. Now uses merit-based public competition (concurso público de méritos).
4. **Senate seats (108)**: Includes 5 transitional FARC/Comunes seats that expire after 2022-2026 period. Should note transitional nature.

### 05-nacionalidad.md
5. **CRITICAL: Residency for Latin Americans**: Says 1 year — WRONG under Ley 2332 de 2023. Now 5 years general, 2 years with family ties/reciprocity, 1 year only for stateless.
6. **Art. 100 citation**: Says restrictions on naturalized citizens come from Art. 100 — WRONG. Art. 100 is about foreigners' rights. Restrictions are in various articles + Ley 2332.

### 01-estructura-del-estado.md
7. **Districts list incomplete**: Only lists 5 districts; Acto Legislativo 02 de 2020 created additional districts (Mitú, Quibdó, Tumaco, etc.).

## HISTORY

### 02-independencia.md
8. **Florero incident**: Says "hermanos Francisco y Antonio Morales pidieron prestado un florero" — most sources say it was Luis de Rubio who asked. Morales brothers were agitators, not the requesters.

### 03-republica.md
9. **M-19 founded "1970"**: Operational founding was 1974 (public debut Jan 17, 1974). 1970 is the election that inspired the name.

### 04-personajes-importantes.md
10. **Policarpa on 10,000 peso bill**: Says "Actualmente aparece en el billete de 10.000 pesos" — OUTDATED. Current bill features Virginia Gutiérrez de Pineda.
11. **Gaitán birth year**: File says 1903; Banco de la República says 1898. Scholarly dispute.

## GEOGRAPHY

### 03-departamentos-y-capitales.md
12. **CRITICAL: Missing San Andrés y Providencia**: Only 31 departments listed. The 32nd department (Archipiélago de San Andrés, Providencia y Santa Catalina, capital: San Andrés) is completely absent.

## CULTURE

### 01-simbolos-nacionales.md
13. **Coat of arms sections WRONG**: Pomegranate/cornucopias are in TOP section (not middle). MIDDLE section has Phrygian cap (gorro frigio) — completely omitted. Bottom is correct (ships/Isthmus).
14. **National flower colors**: Says "blanco, morado y amarillo" evoke the flag — but flag is yellow, blue, red. The orchid lip has yellow/blue/red tones.
15. **Miranda's flag date**: File says 1807; most sources say 1801 or 1806.

### 03-fiestas-y-patrimonio.md
16. **Las Lajas listed as UNESCO World Heritage**: NOT a UNESCO site. It's a National Monument.
17. **Missing 4 UNESCO sites**: Mompox (1995), Los Katíos (1994), Malpelo (2006), Chiribiquete (2018).
18. **Missing Feria de Cali** from festivals.
19. **Missing UNESCO inscription years** for Popayán (2009), Negros y Blancos (2009), Vallenato (2015).
